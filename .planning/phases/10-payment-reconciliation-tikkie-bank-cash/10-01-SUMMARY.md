# Phase 10: Payment Reconciliation (Tikkie Open, Bank Transfers, Cash)

**Status:** Not planned yet

## Summary

This phase adds unified payment reconciliation to handle:

- Open Tikkie payments (event-wide payment link with payer name matching)
- Bank transfer manual entry
- Cash manual entry
- Auto-matching by payer name → buyer name
- Manual assignment for unresolved payments

## Key Features

1. **Payment Table** - Unified model for all payment sources
2. **Open Tikkie Integration** - Poll incoming payments, match by payer name
3. **Manual Bank Transfer Entry** - Form to add payments with order search
4. **Manual Cash Entry** - Form to record cash payments
5. **Reconciliation Dashboard** - View unassigned/partial/overpayment states
